Card 001 · Instructions

How to use the DTZ speaking-card set

These 53 mobile pages supplement the printed cards with complete tasks, useful phrases, model answers, dialogues and vocabulary.

1

Read the task

First read only the front of the card and understand the topic and key points.

2

Speak freely

Speak aloud without immediately looking at the solution. Practise alone or with another person.

3

Check your answer

Then use the back of the card and this website to complete and improve your answer.

4

Practise again

Complete the quick exercise and repeat the task with new information.

Interactive speaking practice

Test ChatGPT Voice Mode

First test copying, opening and speaking with three simple introductory questions.

Your own ChatGPT account is recommended

Sign in to your own ChatGPT account so that Voice Mode and the chat work reliably. A free ChatGPT account is sufficient, but Voice Mode usage limits vary by account, device and region.

  1. Tap the button. The practice prompt is copied and ChatGPT opens. Sign in to your ChatGPT account if necessary.
  2. Start Voice Mode in ChatGPT first.
  3. Paste the copied prompt into the Voice Mode chat and send it.
  4. Then begin the speaking exercise.
  5. When you want to finish the exercise and receive feedback, say: Die Übung ist beendet. Bitte gib mir jetzt Feedback.
Copy prompt & open ChatGPT

Show or manually copy the prompt

ChatGPT is an external service. You may need to sign in and allow microphone access for Voice Mode. Available features depend on your device and account. This exercise is not an official exam assessment.
